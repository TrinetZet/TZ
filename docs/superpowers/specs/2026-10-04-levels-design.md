# ClientBridge: three useful practice levels

Purpose: replace the 100%-effective longest-answer shortcut with context-sensitive business English practice, retaining existing local learning data and native features.

## Learning model
12 existing mission topics, each with Beginner / Advanced / Expert versions of three distinct decisions: 36 missions, 108 decisions, 324 authored choices. Beginner emphasises explicit requests, understanding and safe commitments. Advanced requires prioritising dependencies, trade-offs and evidence. Expert requires pragmatic interpretation, face-saving dissent, calibrated uncertainty and reciprocal negotiation, not wordiness. Each choice receives context-specific explanation and 0–3 points in Context judgment, Relationship & tone, Action & risk; 27 points per mission. The unique best response scores 9, alternatives are credible partial successes with substantive risks. Before answering, a collapsed optional hint avoids giving away the answer; after answering, compare all three rationales and rehearse an ungraded response aloud. No free-text AI scoring or CEFR/native proficiency certification.

## Content safeguards
Across every level, best responses span short/middle/long length ranks; no rank dominates. Source best positions are balanced. Audit character and word longest/shortest strategies including ties, fixed positions, strict-best rates and partial-credit scores; each must remain below 80%, preventing trivial perfection. Uniform permutation for every decision, generated once per attempt, persisted; stored answers refer to canonical choice indices. Randomness never changes feedback mapping or saved results.

## UI
Persistent three-button difficulty selector on Practice room and My progress; difficulty pill on mission/result. Every level has all twelve topics and separate resume/retry. Switching does not reset another level; selecting a new level from a mission returns to its dashboard. Profiles show all three level summaries plus selected level dimensions, attempts and completed count. No data: Not enough practice, not invented zero. At least three completed missions required for an emerging dimension signal; mastery/needs-practice wording explicitly describes practice evidence. Recommend partial mission first, then an unseen mission relevant to lowest dimension, then weakest completed mission. Legacy results visible in expandable archive with original rubric and drafts retained. History entries show their own immutable attempt detail, not silently current result.
